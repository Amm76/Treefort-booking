FAQ

Helpful Details Before You Visit

Do you accept walk-ins or is booking required? +

Walk-ins are always welcome! For weekend peak hours or groups of 4+, we recommend booking via WhatsApp at least 1 hour ahead so we can hold a table for you.

Is Treefort Cafe pork-free and Muslim-friendly? +

Yes, our kitchen is 100% pork-free and alcohol-free, using ingredients sourced from Halal-certified suppliers.

Is parking available nearby? +

Yes, public street parking and nearby open-air parking bays are available right around the cafe.

Can we host a birthday or private gathering here? +

Definitely! Select 'Group / Private Event' in the booking form with your preferred date and headcount, and our team will assist with seating and platter arrangements.
